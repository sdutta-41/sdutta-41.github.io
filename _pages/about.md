Hello! I am an Assistant Teaching Professor of English & Writing at the University of Tampa. 

Previously, I was a Brittain Postdoctoral Fellow and the Assistant Director of the Writing & Communication Program at Georgia Tech. I did my PhD in English at the University of Miami (2021). My research and teaching incorporate advancements in critical pedagogy, cultural rhetorics, and writing across the curriculum.

I have presented my scholarly work at national and regional conferences like MLA, CCCC, SAMLA, NeMLA, & DHSI-Canada. I have also published papers at venues like *\[Inter\]Sections: The American Studies Journal* and the *Impact Journal*.  For more information on my teaching and research, please see my blog.

Link to my current [CV](assets/pdf/Dutta_CV_may25.pdf)

When not dabbling in academia, I love to cook Indian food with my husband, play with my toddler, and look for new hikes & parks in the city.
